Your privacy, simply

This demo does not send form entries or ask for payment details. Saved itineraries are downloaded to your own device. External image and font providers receive standard requests when you are online.

A few helpful answers.

Is this a live booking platform?

This is an interactive SIH prototype. You can explore stays, create itineraries and try booking flows, but no reservations or payments are made.

Can I use Tripzy without an internet connection?

Yes. Open index.html to use the demo pages, filters and planner offline. Photography and web fonts need a connection; captions and system fonts remain available.

How does the trip planner work?

This prototype uses curated activities and your selected dates, budget, interests, pace and traveler count. It demonstrates the intended experience without calling an AI service.

What happens when I press SOS?

The prototype asks for confirmation and displays a demo notice. It does not place calls, request your location or contact anyone.

How do local guides join Tripzy?

The launch version will include partner onboarding and verification. For this prototype, all guide profiles are fictional examples.
