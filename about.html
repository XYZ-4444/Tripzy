Redefining travel with technology.

India is full of extraordinary places. We believe discovering them should feel simple, personal and deeply local.

A connected tourism ecosystem

A better journey.
For everyone.

Tripzy connects the traveler dreaming of a new adventure with the people who make a destination special: independent hoteliers, knowledgeable guides and local food businesses.

Our prototype brings these pieces into one approachable experience. The long-term vision is to combine verified information, personalised planning and accessible support.
